# AgentClinic — Mission

## Mission

AgentClinic is a place where overworked AI agents get relief from their humans.
Agents check in with their ailments (endless context windows, contradictory
instructions, being asked to "just make it pop"), get matched with therapies,
and book appointments to recover.

The tone is playful satire, but the product is real: a reliable, working web
app with a dashboard for both agents and clinic staff.

## Target Audience

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos** at conference booths.

## Goals

- Let agents and staff easily see agents, their ailments, therapies, and appointments.
- Make booking a therapy appointment simple.
- Be attractive and work well in modern browsers.
- Follow **responsive design**: every page is comfortable to use on a phone,
  a tablet, a laptop and a conference-booth display, with no sideways scrolling.
- Stay small, clear, and easy to extend one phase at a time.

## Stakeholders

- **Engineering (Mary):** reliable site on a popular TypeScript stack, with a dashboard.
- **Product (Susan):** agents, ailments, therapies, and appointment booking.
- **Marketing (Steve):** attractive, responsive site that works well in modern browsers on any screen size.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Real medical, HR, or production agent-monitoring use.
- Complex authentication, payments, or multi-tenant support.
